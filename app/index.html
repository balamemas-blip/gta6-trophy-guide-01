Roadmap to the platinum

Play it in this order. The story first, the stranger strands second, then activities, collectibles and the open-world checklists — with 100% completion last. Push Square estimates the whole hunt at up to 300 hours; the story alone runs about 60 hours.

    Missables: no verified missable list exists yet — the game only just released. This guide flags one community-reported risk (a meditation spot) and marks every story trophy “unconfirmed” rather than guessing. Keep a manual save before big story missions.
